Assurance Workflow

1
Vendor / AI Tool Intake
Capture vendor, data type, AI usage, integrations, permissions, and requester.
2
Risk Classification
Classify by data sensitivity, access level, AI behavior, and business criticality.
3
Control Mapping
Map findings to SOC 2, ISO 42001 concepts, privacy, security, and common controls.
4
Evidence Validation
Check whether evidence is complete, current, source-backed, and consistent.
5
Risk Register Entry
Turn failures into owned risks, exceptions, due dates, and compensating actions.
6
ServiceNow Routing
Generate remediation payloads and track closure instead of leaving findings in a spreadsheet.

Cost Model Assumptions

These are demo assumptions, not audited savings claims. In a real environment, the inputs would come from intake records, GRC tool activity, ServiceNow timestamps, questionnaire logs, and evidence validation results.

How the Cost Is Calculated

Manual Labor Cost
manual touches x avg minutes x loaded hourly rate
Evidence Rework Cost
failed evidence x rework minutes x loaded hourly rate
Net Avoided Effort
manual cost avoided - runtime - maintenance

Executive Readout

What changed?
Fewer manual touches, faster reviews, cleaner evidence, clearer ownership, and less status reconciliation.
What still matters?
High-risk AI vendors, duplicate evidence locations, stale documentation, and unresolved remediation.
Why fund it?
It reduces the total cost of assurance while improving velocity, evidence reliability, and risk visibility.
